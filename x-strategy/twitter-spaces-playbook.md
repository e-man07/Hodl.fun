# Hodl.fun Twitter Spaces Playbook

> Twitter Spaces are a major engagement driver. This playbook covers everything from planning to execution.

---

## Why Spaces Matter

- **Algorithm boost**: Live audio content gets prioritized in notifications
- **Community depth**: Voice builds trust faster than text
- **Content multiplier**: One Space = multiple content pieces
- **Relationship building**: Co-hosts expand your reach
- **Real-time engagement**: Direct audience interaction

## Space Formats

### Format 1: Office Hours (Weekly Staple)

**Duration:** 30-45 minutes
**Hosts:** 1-2 team members
**Audience:** Open to all

**Structure:**
```
0-5 min:   Welcome, quick intro, what we'll cover
5-15 min:  Weekly update (new features, stats, highlights)
15-35 min: Open Q&A (bring up listeners)
35-45 min: Tease upcoming, CTAs, close
```

**Topics to Cover:**
- Testnet stats and highlights
- Feature updates
- Community questions
- Token spotlights
- Bug reports and fixes
- Upcoming plans

**Announcement Post:**
```
hodl.fun office hours - LIVE in 24 hours

thursday 3pm EST

what we'll cover:
• this week's testnet stats
• [specific update]
• your questions

set a reminder 👇

https://twitter.com/i/spaces/[id]
```

### Format 2: Degen Roundtable

**Duration:** 45-60 minutes
**Hosts:** Team + 3-5 active community members
**Audience:** Engaged testnet users

**Structure:**
```
0-5 min:   Intros
5-20 min:  Each panelist shares their hodl.fun experience
20-40 min: Discussion: what's working, what's not
40-55 min: Audience questions
55-60 min: Wrap up, next steps
```

**Panelist Selection Criteria:**
- Active on testnet (launched token or significant trading)
- Active on Twitter
- Genuine enthusiasm (not paid shills)
- Diverse perspectives

**Announcement Post:**
```
degen roundtable - LIVE thursday

5 of the most active hodl.fun testnet users share:
• what they've learned
• what's working
• honest feedback

featuring:
@user1
@user2
@user3
@user4
@user5

set reminder: [link]
```

### Format 3: Token Creator Spotlight

**Duration:** 30-45 minutes
**Hosts:** Team + 1-2 successful token creators
**Audience:** Aspiring creators

**Structure:**
```
0-5 min:   Host intro, guest intro
5-15 min:  Guest's story (why they launched, what happened)
15-30 min: Tactical breakdown (what worked, what didn't)
30-40 min: Audience questions for guest
40-45 min: Close with guest's advice for new creators
```

**Guest Selection:**
- Launched successful token on hodl.fun
- Willing to share real numbers/experience
- Good speaker (test via DM call first)
- Has audience overlap

**Announcement Post:**
```
token creator spotlight

@[creator] launched $[TICKER] on hodl.fun

• [X] holders in [X] hours
• [X]x from launch
• brought their whole community

thursday 3pm EST - they're sharing exactly how they did it

set reminder: [link]
```

### Format 4: CT Hot Takes

**Duration:** 30-45 minutes
**Hosts:** Team + 1-2 CT personalities
**Audience:** Broader crypto audience

**Structure:**
```
0-5 min:   Intros
5-30 min:  React to this week's crypto news/trends
30-40 min: Audience questions/takes
40-45 min: Wrap up
```

**Topics:**
- Major crypto news
- Memecoin trends (TikTok coins, etc.)
- Chain wars
- Launchpad competition
- Market conditions

**Co-Host Selection:**
- CT accounts with 10k+ followers
- Active in memecoin/launchpad space
- Willing to have opinions
- Not competitors

---

### Format 5: AMA (For Big Announcements)

**Duration:** 60 minutes
**Hosts:** Founders/core team
**Audience:** Maximum reach

**Structure:**
```
0-10 min:  Big announcement reveal
10-30 min: Deep dive on announcement
30-55 min: Community questions
55-60 min: What's next, CTAs
```

**When to Use:**

- [ ] Prepare talking points
- [ ] Create reminder post for 24 hours before

### 24 Hours Before
- [ ] Post reminder with link

- [ ] Notes/talking points ready
- [ ] Phone on do not disturb

### 5 Minutes Before
- [ ] Start Space early (people join early)
- [ ] Welcome early arrivals
- [ ] Quick audio check with co-hosts

## During Space Best Practices

### Hosting Tips
- **Energy**: Sound excited, not monotone
- **Pacing**: Don't rush, let silence happen
- **Inclusion**: Bring up listeners regularly
- **Names**: Use people's names/handles
- **Summaries**: Recap key points periodically

### Bringing Up Listeners
- "Great question from @[handle]..."
- "I see [handle] requested to speak, let's bring them up"
- "Anyone in the audience want to add to that?"

### Handling Difficult Situations

**Dead air:**
- "While we wait for questions, let me share..."
- Have backup topics ready

**Trolls/bad actors:**
- Mute immediately, don't engage
- "We're going to move on..."

**Technical issues:**
- "Having some audio issues, one sec"
- If persistent, end and reschedule

**Off-topic questions:**
- "Great question but let's save that for another time"
- "DM us about that, happy to discuss"

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **No preparation** - Always have talking points
2. **Too long** - 30-45 min is ideal, 60 max
3. **Monologuing** - Involve audience every 10 min
4. **Bad audio** - Test before, use headphones
5. **Wrong time** - Stick to peak CT hours
6. **No follow-up** - Always post recap content
7. **Ignoring chat** - Have someone monitor while you talk
8. **No CTA** - End with clear next step

## Equipment Recommendations

### Minimum Viable Setup
- Quiet room
- Wired earbuds with mic
- Phone on do not disturb

### Better Setup
- USB microphone (Blue Yeti, etc.)
- Headphones (not earbuds)
- Ring light (if using camera)
- Second device to monitor chat

### Pro Setup
- XLR microphone + audio interface
- Acoustic treatment
- Dedicated streaming computer
- Producer/moderator

**Start minimum, upgrade as audience grows**

## Spaces vs. Other Formats

| Format | Pros | Cons | When to Use |
|--------|------|------|-------------|
| Spaces | Real-time, algorithm boost | Requires scheduling | Weekly engagement |
| Pre-recorded | Polished, shareable | No live interaction | Educational content |
| Video call clips | Visual, personal | More production | Announcements |

**Spaces are best for community building and real-time engagement.**
