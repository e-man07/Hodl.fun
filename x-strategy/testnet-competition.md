# Hodl.fun Testnet Competition Framework

## Overview

Competitions drive engagement, create content opportunities, and build community. This framework provides ready-to-launch competition formats.

---

## Competition Types

| Type | Duration | Difficulty | Content Created | Best For |
|------|----------|------------|-----------------|----------|
| Trading Competition | 1 week | Low | Leaderboards, highlights | Volume + engagement |
| Launch Competition | 1 week | Medium | Token showcases | Creator acquisition |
| Meme Competition | 3-5 days | Low | UGC memes | Awareness + fun |
| Referral Competition | 2 weeks | Medium | Social sharing | Growth hacking |
| Community Challenge | Varies | Varies | Diverse content | Engagement |

## Competition Operations

### Pre-Competition Checklist
- [ ] Finalize rules and prizes
- [ ] Set up tracking (leaderboard, spreadsheet, or on-chain)
- [ ] Prepare announcement content
- [ ] Schedule reminder posts
- [ ] Coordinate with team for engagement
- [ ] Set up prize distribution method

### During Competition
- [ ] Daily leaderboard updates
- [ ] Highlight interesting participants
- [ ] Engage with all competition-related posts
- [ ] Address questions/issues quickly
- [ ] Build hype for close races

### Post-Competition
- [ ] Announce winners publicly
- [ ] Distribute prizes within 48 hours
- [ ] Thank all participants
- [ ] Share stats/highlights
- [ ] Tease next competition

---

## Anti-Gaming Measures

### Sybil Prevention
- Minimum activity thresholds
- Manual review of suspicious patterns
- Require social verification for large prizes
- IP/wallet clustering detection (if possible)

### Fair Play Rules
- One entry per person (for applicable competitions)
- Self-trading doesn't count
- Wash trading disqualifies
- Team reserves right to disqualify suspicious activity

### Transparency
- Public leaderboards
- Clear rules posted upfront
- Disqualification reasons shared (without revealing detection methods)

## Budget Considerations

### Low Budget Options
- Whitelist spots (free, high perceived value)
- Social recognition/badges
- Feature placement
- Early access

### Medium Budget Options
- Protocol tokens (PUSH)
- NFTs
- Merch

### High Budget Options
- Cash prizes
- Hardware wallets
- Conference tickets
- Larger token allocations

**Recommendation:** Start with whitelist + small token amounts. Increase as traction grows.
