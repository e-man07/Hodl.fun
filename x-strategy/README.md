## Algorithm Cheat Sheet

### What Drives Reach (Prioritize These)

| Signal | Weight | Action |
|--------|--------|--------|
| **Replies** | 27x vs likes | Ask questions, spark debate |
| **OP-Reply interaction** | 75x multiplier | Reply to every comment |
| **Video** | High priority | 3 native videos/week |
| **Dwell time** | High | Images, threads, engaging hooks |

### What Kills Reach (Avoid)

| Signal | Impact | Avoid |
|--------|--------|-------|
| Links in post body | Penalty | Put in replies |
| Mutes/blocks | Severe | Don't spam |
| No early engagement | Deprioritized | Coordinate team engagement |
| Old meme formats | Signals out-of-touch | Use current formats |

## Daily Structure

### Morning (9-11 AM EST)
- Post primary content
- Reply to ALL overnight comments
- 10 bridge complaint replies

### Midday (12-2 PM EST)
- Post secondary content
- Engage on target accounts
- Check for reactive opportunities

### Evening (6-9 PM EST)
- Post meme/entertainment
- Community engagement
- Reply to day's comments

### Daily Reply Targets (NON-NEGOTIABLE)

| Activity | Target | Why |
|----------|--------|-----|
| Reply to own comments | 100% | 75x algorithm boost |
| Bridge complaint replies | 10 | Target audience |
| Launchpad discussions | 5 | Relevance |
| Big account replies | 5 | Visibility |
| **Total** | **25-30/day** | |
