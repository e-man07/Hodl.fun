## Video Production Guide

### Equipment Tiers

**Tier 1: Minimum (Start Here)**
- Phone camera
- Natural lighting
- Built-in screen recording
- CapCut for editing (free)

**Tier 2: Better**
- Ring light
- External microphone
- Loom for screen recording
- CapCut Pro or Premiere Rush

**Tier 3: Professional**
- Mirrorless camera
- Softbox lighting
- Dedicated microphone
- After Effects + Premiere Pro

**Recommendation:** Start Tier 1, upgrade as content performs

### Screen Recording Tips

1. **Clean your desktop** - Hide sensitive info, close notifications
2. **Increase cursor size** - Easier to follow
3. **Slow mouse movements** - Don't rush
4. **Record at 1080p minimum**
5. **Use countdown before action** - Edit out later

### Editing Best Practices

- **Cut dead space** - No waiting, loading, hesitation
- **Add text overlays** - Key info should be readable on mute
- **Use captions** - 80% watch without sound
- **Punchy start** - First 2 seconds determine watch
- **Clear end** - CTA or loop point

## Video Formats by Platform

### X/Twitter Native

**Specs:**
- Aspect ratio: 16:9 (landscape) or 1:1 (square)
- Length: 15-60 seconds optimal
- File size: Under 512MB
- Resolution: 1080p

**Best practices:**
- Upload native (not YouTube links)
- Add captions
- Strong thumbnail frame
- Hook in first 2 seconds

### Vertical Video (Future - TikTok/Reels)

**Specs:**
- Aspect ratio: 9:16
- Length: 15-60 seconds
- Resolution: 1080x1920

**Best practices:**
- Text in safe zone (not edges)
- Trending audio when relevant
- Quick cuts, high energy
- Hook immediately

## Hooks That Work for Video

First 2 seconds determine everything.

### Effective Video Hooks

**Question hooks:**
- "ever lost money to bridge fees?"
- "want to trade from any chain?"
- "what if bridging didn't exist?"

**Statement hooks:**
- "8 seconds. that's it."
- "i never bridge anymore"
- "this changes everything"

**Action hooks:**
- Start mid-action (already clicking)
- Movement in first frame
- Something happening immediately

### Hooks to Avoid

- Logos/intros (skip immediately)
- "Hey guys..." (skip immediately)
- Slow fade ins
- Anything static

## Quick Production Workflow

### 30-Minute Video Creation

1. **5 min**: Plan (outline what to show)
2. **10 min**: Record (screen capture + any footage)
3. **10 min**: Edit (cut, add text, captions)
4. **5 min**: Review + post

### Batch Production (Recommended)

Record 3-5 videos in one session:
- Same setup, different content
- Edit in batches
- Schedule throughout week

## Common Video Mistakes

1. **Too long** - Keep under 60 seconds
2. **Slow start** - Hook in first 2 seconds
3. **No captions** - 80% watch muted
4. **Poor audio** - Rather have no audio than bad audio
5. **Overproduced** - Authenticity > polish
6. **No CTA** - Tell them what to do
7. **YouTube links** - Upload native to X
8. **Boring thumbnail** - First frame matters
