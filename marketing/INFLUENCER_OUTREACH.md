# Influencer Outreach Strategy & Tracker

## Influencer Tier Definitions

| Tier | Followers | Engagement | Cost Range | Use Case |
|------|-----------|------------|------------|----------|
| **Nano** | 1K-10K | 5-10% | $50-200 | Seed buzz, authentic reviews |
| **Micro** | 10K-100K | 3-5% | $200-1,000 | Best ROI, community trust |
| **Mid** | 100K-500K | 1-3% | $1,000-5,000 | Reach expansion |
| **Macro** | 500K+ | <1% | $5,000+ | Mainstream awareness |

**Recommended Strategy:** Start with Nano → Micro cascade, add selective Mid-tier for amplification.

## Outreach Templates

### Initial Outreach (Cold)

```

2. Share the experience with your audience

Happy to cover any costs + [specific incentive].

No strings. Just think your audience would dig the creator economics angle.

[Link to platform]

Let me know if interested.

[Your name]
```


```
Hey [name],

Thanks for showing interest in Hodl.fun!

Here's what I'm thinking:

**What you'd do:**
- Create a token on the platform (60 seconds)
- Share the experience (thread, video, or whatever fits your style)

- Your token featured on our channels

**Timeline:**
- [Proposed date range]

Sound good? Happy to hop on a call if easier.

[Your name]
```

### Thank You (Post-Collaboration)

## Outreach Tracking

### Status Key
- **Not contacted** - On list, not reached out
- **Contacted** - Initial message sent
- **Responded** - Got a reply
- **Negotiating** - Discussing terms
- **Agreed** - Deal confirmed
- **Live** - Content published
- **Completed** - Paid and done
- **Declined** - Said no
- **No response** - No reply after follow-up

### Week 1 Outreach Targets

- [ ] Identify 3 Mid-tier prospects

### Week 2 Outreach Targets
- [ ] Follow up with Week 1 contacts
- [ ] Contact 10 more Nano influencers

- [ ] First content live

## Content Requirements

### Minimum Expectations
- Honest/authentic take
- Disclosure of sponsorship (FTC compliance)
- Link to platform
- No competitor bashing

### Preferred Content
- Actual platform usage (not just talking about it)
- Screen recording/screenshots
- Personal experience narrative
- Call to action for audience

### No-Go Content
- Guaranteed returns claims
- Price predictions
- False information
- Undisclosed sponsorship

## Finding New Influencers

### Search Methods
1. **Twitter search:** "pump.fun" OR "token launch" OR "bonding curve"
2. **Follower analysis:** Who do target users follow?
3. **Competitor tags:** Who tagged Pump.fun, LetsBonk, etc.?
4. **Push community:** Active Push Protocol participants
5. **Crypto lists:** Crypto Twitter lists from established accounts

### Qualification Criteria
- [ ] Engagement rate > 2%
- [ ] Authentic followers (no bots)
- [ ] Relevant audience
- [ ] No major controversies
- [ ] Active in last 7 days
- [ ] Content quality acceptable

### Red Flags
- Sudden follower spikes (bought followers)
- Engagement doesn't match follower count
- Constant paid promotions (shill account)
- History of promoting rugs/scams
- Inactive or sporadic posting

## Relationship Building

### Long-term Value
Don't treat influencers as one-time transactions. Build relationships:

1. **Engage before pitching** - Like, reply, RT their content first
2. **Provide value** - Share alpha, insights, early access
3. **Be responsive** - Quick replies, helpful support
4. **Celebrate their wins** - Amplify their successes
5. **Stay in touch** - Periodic check-ins, not just when you need something

### Ambassador Pipeline
Best influencers → Long-term ambassadors

**Ambassador Benefits:**
- Monthly retainer
- Exclusive alpha
- Revenue share
- Direct input on features
- Co-marketing opportunities
