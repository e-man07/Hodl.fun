## Attribution Tracking

### UTM Parameters
Use consistent UTM parameters for all links:

```
?utm_source=[platform]&utm_medium=[type]&utm_campaign=[campaign]
```

**Sources:**
- twitter
- discord
- telegram
- influencer-[name]
- email
- direct

**Mediums:**
- organic
- paid
- referral
- social

**Campaigns:**
- launch-week
- genesis-creator
- trading-competition
- [custom]

### Attribution Examples
```
thehodl.fun?utm_source=twitter&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=launch-week
thehodl.fun?utm_source=influencer-cryptoguy&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=week2
```

## Red Flags to Watch

### Platform Health
- Token creation drops >50% day-over-day
- No trades on new tokens
- Creator complaints increasing
- Technical issues reported

### Social Health
- Engagement rate drops <1%
- Follower growth stalls
- Negative sentiment increases
- Community activity drops

### Campaign Health
- Influencer content underperforms
- Conversion rate drops
- Cost per acquisition rises
- ROI turns negative

---

## Success Definitions

### Week 1 Success
- Platform works without major issues
- Initial community established
- First tokens created by external users
- Positive sentiment

### Week 2 Success
- Organic growth begins
- Creator success stories emerge
- Trading activity increases
- Influencer content goes live

### Week 3 Success
- Social proof established
- Community self-sustaining activity
- External mentions/press
- Multiple successful tokens

### Week 4 Success
- Clear growth trajectory
- Strong community engagement
- Partnership/collaboration interest
- Ready for mainnet push
